# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.
Contributors must discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, or sustained disruption. Good-faith technical
dissent is welcome and must be preserved in consequential decision records.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub's
private reporting channel. Maintainers will protect confidentiality where
possible, investigate proportionately, disclose conflicts, and may warn, limit,
or remove participation. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
